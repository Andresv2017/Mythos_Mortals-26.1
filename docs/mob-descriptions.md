# Mythos & Mortals — Mob Descriptions

## 🛡️ Athenian Warrior
A spear-and-shield hoplite who comes for you the moment he sees you and fights at arm's length.

* Fights on a guard cycle: he closes the distance, brings the shield up, strafes around you for a few seconds, and only breaks the pose to thrust.
* The spear gives him reach — he can hit you from outside where you can hit him, so getting inside his guard means walking through the point first.
* Alternates two thrusts, and the damage lands on the impact frame of the swing rather than on contact, so both can be read and stepped out of.
* The raised shield actually blocks. Keep hitting it and his poise gives out — the guard break has its own sound, and that is your window.
* Hostile on sight. He goes after husks too, but a player in range always comes first — and if you hit him, whoever hit him is who he answers.
* Drops bone, and sometimes the kit he was carrying: his spear or his helmet, both scarred and half-spent from the campaign he died on.

Spawns in: Plains, in ones and twos.

## 🔴 Spartan
The same discipline with a sword, hitting harder and covering less.

* Trained on the same guard cycle as the Athenian — shield up, circle, commit — but he hits noticeably harder with both swings.
* His guard blocks; his attacks don't. The shield stays down through the whole swing, so the moment he commits is the moment he is open.
* Shorter reach than the Athenian's spear, which means he has to come to you — and closing is when he is most exposed.
* Hostile on sight, like the Athenian, and just as happy to take a husk apart on the way to you.
* Drops his kit the same way: a worn sword or a battered helmet, whichever he had left.

Spawns in: Savanna and Savanna Plateau, in ones and twos.

## 🦅 Arpy
A peak-nesting raptor-woman that sleeps on her crag until someone climbs too close, then hunts by falling out of the sky.

* She doesn't chase — she stoops. She swings wide, climbs above you, lines the dive up until she is facing you head-on, drops, rakes you with the talons, and pulls up before she hits the ground.
* Every pass has to be set up again from altitude, so a dive you dodge buys you a few seconds of clear sky. Fighting her is a rhythm, not a brawl.
* She roosts at her nest and stays on the ground there until a player comes within earshot. Wake her and she is in the air for the rest of the fight.
* The ones guarding a nest come armoured: tougher, better protected and hitting harder than the ones circling the peaks.
* Hostile on sight. She takes cattle when there is nothing better on the mountain, but a player on her peak outranks the herd every time.
* Drops Arpy Feathers.

Spawns in: Jagged Peaks, Stony Peaks and Windswept Hills, plus the Arpy Nests that generate on true summits — it takes a real peak with a real drop around it, not just a tall hill.

## 🕊️ Pegasus
The winged horse of the high meadows — it won't let you near it, and it won't let you off once it does.

* Wild ones bolt the instant a standing player gets close and don't stop for a long way. Crouch or you will never get within reach of one.
* Feed it apples while crouched. Each one it accepts warms it to you and improves the odds; a golden apple wins it over on the spot.
* The moment it accepts you it takes off with you aboard and tries to throw you, high above the ground. Get the Athena Bridle onto it while it bucks and it is yours for good — fail, and it drops you and goes wild again. That bridle comes off a Minotaur, so a pegasus is never the first thing you tame.
* Once tamed it carries a saddle, its own barding and its own inventory, and it flies where you look, banking into every turn.
* Sprinting is a real second gear. On top of that, Wind Surge launches it forward hard enough to become a battering ram: everything it plows through takes the hit and gets thrown aside.
* It has no attack of its own. The only weapon a pegasus has is its own speed.

Spawns in: Meadow, Grove, Jagged Peaks, Stony Peaks and Windswept Hills — always alone, always high up under open sky. Rare.

## 🦉 Owl of Athena
A bronze statue that wakes into a companion, and eventually into a second pair of eyes you can fly yourself.

* It doesn't spawn anywhere. You find the statue, and you bring it a Bronze Core — which means killing a Minotaur, or getting very lucky in the ruins of Athens.
* It bonds to whoever wakes it, permanently. Only its owner can command it, and it never lands on its own: empty hand to call it to your arm, again to send it back up.
* It defends you on its own initiative, diving on whatever threatens you and breaking off before it chases into someone else's fight.
* Feed it copper to make it permanently tougher, and bronze to patch it up mid-fight.
* **Athena's Ocular** unlocks Athena's Sight: you leave your body standing where it is and fly the owl yourself. Attack to swoop; look at something and mark it so it glows for everyone. It refuses to activate while the owl is fighting or while anything hostile is near your body — the one thing it will never do is scout your way out of an ambush.
* **Sonic Screech** gives it a screech that hits at range, staggers what it hits and throws it back. The owl uses it on its own too, alternating screeches with dives depending on how far the target is.
* Aim a spyglass at something far off and click, and the owl goes and kills it while you stay on the ground.

Found in: nowhere naturally. It comes out of an Owl Statue, and the key comes out of a Minotaur.

## 🐂 Minotaur
The bull-headed guardian of the arena beneath the plains — it roars once when it sees you, then closes the distance on its own terms.

* It never flinches. There is no hurt reaction, no stagger, no interrupt — whatever it has started, it finishes.
* Fixed opener: the moment it marks you it stops dead and roars, then charges if you gave it room. That roar is the only free hit you are ever handed.
* The charge crosses the arena and sends you flying. Put a wall behind you instead and it rams the stone and knocks itself senseless — that is the punish window the whole fight is built around.
* Up close it works a combo: a sweep, usually chained into a heavier second, and sometimes finished with either a shove that clears you off it or an overhead that comes down hard enough to break a raised shield outright.
* The overhead has a long telegraph and a long reach, and you cannot back out of it. You dodge that one sideways.
* It attacks everything alive nearby, soldiers included, and it remembers who hit it.
* Every one of them gives up a key when it dies — either the Bronze Core that wakes the Owl of Athena, or the Athena Bridle that breaks a pegasus. Which one is out of your hands, so the second one you need means finding another arena.

Found in: the Minotaur Arena, deep underground beneath Plains, Sunflower Plains, savannas and Dripstone Caves. One per arena.
